## 1.0.0

### Added

- Internet Down and Restored, New Device, and opt-in Device Offline alerts to PingKit on your
  iPhone.
- The status page in the sidebar, on a port of its own that answers only Home Assistant.
- Pairing by claim code.
- A custom AppArmor profile, for a security rating of 7 of 8.
- With Mosquitto installed, the agent finds your broker by itself: nothing to type.
